About Solivage Travels

Embark on Journeys That Nourish the Soul

Founded in Nairobi in 2020, Solivage Travels is a boutique Kenyan travel company redefining adventure with a touch of soulful serenity. We believe travel isn't just about destinations—it's about the stories you carry home, the connections you forge, and the quiet moments that recharge your spirit.

As proud stewards of Kenya's wild beauty, we craft bespoke experiences that blend comfort, culture, and adventure—ensuring every trip leaves you renewed.
